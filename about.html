About Connect 4

Connect 4 is a classic two-player connection game in which the players take turns dropping colored discs from the top into a vertically suspended grid.

The objective is to be the first to form a horizontal, vertical, or diagonal line of four of one's own discs. This timeless game has been a favorite for generations, challenging players with its simple yet strategic gameplay.

Whether you're playing against a friend or challenging our AI, Connect 4 offers endless hours of fun and strategic thinking. Plan your moves carefully and outwit your opponent!
