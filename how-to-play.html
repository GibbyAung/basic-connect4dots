📖 How to Play

Choose Your Column

Click on any column to drop your colored disc. The disc will fall to the lowest available position.

Step 1/5

📋 Quick Rules

  • Select a game mode from the Game Modes page.
  • Players take turns dropping colored discs into the grid.
  • The first player to connect 4 discs in a row wins.
  • If all cells are filled with no winner, it's a draw.
